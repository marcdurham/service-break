UX Improvement: Discovery & Feature Engagement

Problem: The app lacks immediate "Value Realization." After onboarding, the user is dropped onto a map. While powerful, it requires the user to perform an action (scrolling/clicking) before they see why the app is useful.

Solution: Implement a "Quick Start" or "Trending Nearby" tray. Upon first launch, present a small, swipeable card carousel at the bottom of the map showing top-rated bathrooms in the current area.

Proposed Flow

  1. Zero-Click Value: Instead of just a map with pins, show 3 cards for "Highest Rated Nearby" directly on the `MapView` overlay.
  2. Feature Discovery: Use these cards to highlight the features mentioned in onboarding (e.g., a card showing a "5/5 Cleanliness" badge).
  3. Transition to Search: Once the user interacts with a card, transition them into the full `ListView` or `DetailView`.
